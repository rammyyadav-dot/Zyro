What is the cost of a digital marketing package in Patna?

Costs depend on channels, content volume, competition, number of locations and ad spend. Zyrostyle offers Starter, Growth and Scale packages customised after a free consultation; contact us for an exact quote.

Is ad spend included in the package price?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta and is separate from management fees, so you always see exactly where your money goes.

What is the cost of SEO packages in Patna?

SEO pricing depends on website size, competition and whether local, e-commerce or national SEO is needed. Local SEO for a single location is typically the most affordable starting point.

How much do social media marketing (SMM) packages cost in Patna?

SMM package costs depend on the number of platforms, posts and Reels per month and whether Meta Ads management is included.

Is there a long-term contract?

We recommend a minimum of three months to see meaningful results, with clear scope and exit terms written into every agreement.
