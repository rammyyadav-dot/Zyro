How much does SEO cost in Patna?

SEO pricing depends on your website size, competition and goals. Local SEO for one location is the most affordable starting point; e-commerce or multi-city SEO needs more work. We share a fixed monthly quote after a free audit.

Is affordable SEO effective?

Yes, when it focuses on the highest-impact work — Google Business Profile, on-page fixes and local citations — rather than bulk links or fake guarantees.
