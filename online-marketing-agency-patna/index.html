FAQ

Online marketing agency Patna — FAQs.

What does an online marketing agency do?

An online marketing agency builds and grows your business's presence on the internet — website, Google Business Profile, search engine optimisation, social media, online advertising, WhatsApp and email — to bring you more customers.

Is online marketing useful for a small shop in Patna?

Yes. Even a single-location shop benefits from Google Maps visibility, reviews and WhatsApp, because most customers now search online before visiting a local business.

Do I need a website if I have Instagram?

A website gives you a platform you control, ranks on Google and builds trust. Instagram is excellent for engagement, but the strongest results come from using both together.
