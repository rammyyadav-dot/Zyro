FAQ

Ecommerce seo agency Patna — FAQs.

What is e-commerce SEO?

E-commerce SEO is optimising an online store's categories, products, technical setup and content so that its pages rank in Google and attract shoppers ready to buy.

Do you work with Shopify and WooCommerce stores?

Yes. We optimise Shopify, WooCommerce and custom-built stores, including theme-level technical fixes, product schema and Merchant Center feeds.

What is an e-commerce content strategy?

It is a plan for the content that supports sales — category copy, product descriptions, buying guides, comparison pages and email/WhatsApp campaigns — mapped to what shoppers search and when.
