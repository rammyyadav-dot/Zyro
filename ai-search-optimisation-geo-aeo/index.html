FAQ

GEO & AEO — your questions answered.

What is Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) is the practice of structuring useful, credible and clearly attributed information so AI-powered search and discovery systems — such as Google AI Overviews and conversational AI assistants — can accurately understand, summarise and reference your brand and services.

What is Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) means creating clear, direct answers to real customer questions through well-structured service pages, FAQs, explainers and knowledge content, so search engines and voice or AI assistants can surface your answer.

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. GEO and AEO build on strong SEO foundations. AI search systems still rely on crawlable, authoritative, well-structured websites. The most resilient strategy combines all three: SEO, GEO and AEO.

Can you guarantee my business will appear in AI answers?

No one can guarantee inclusion in AI-generated answers, because those systems are controlled by their providers. We improve the factors that make your brand easier to understand and cite: clear entity information, structured data, consistent business listings, credible content and authoritative mentions.

Which businesses in Patna benefit most from GEO and AEO?

Businesses whose customers research before buying — hospitals and clinics, coaching institutes and schools, real-estate developers, B2B companies, travel and hospitality brands — benefit most, because their customers increasingly ask AI tools for recommendations and explanations.
