Implementation

From technical SEO to answer-ready pages.

Get Your Free Consultation
  • 01

    Make priority pages accessible

    We inspect indexability, canonical URLs, internal links and mobile navigation. The implementation brief records each issue, the affected URL and a check to run after the fix.

  • 02

    Answer the buying question first

    Answer engine optimisation (AEO) starts with a direct answer. We add the details a customer needs to act, such as eligibility, a price range you approve or the documents needed for a booking.

  • 03

    Give AI search verifiable context

    For generative engine optimisation (GEO), we connect claims to material you own: named services, current policies, original photos and experience your team can substantiate. We align relevant structured data with visible page content.

  • 04

    Write for the language customers use

    If customers enquire in Hindi or Hinglish, we review real questions with your sales team. We build a separate language page when it helps customers complete the enquiry in that language.

Measurement

Track the journey from search to enquiry.

At kickoff, we agree on the services, pages and conversion events to track. The monthly report compares organic clicks and impressions for priority pages with the previous period, records the changes shipped and names the next action.

A WhatsApp tap shows interest. A confirmed enquiry needs a reply or a record from your team. We report the two separately wherever the data is available, so you can see which pages bring conversations worth following up.

For AI visibility, we can sample an agreed set of questions and record the date, tool and cited page. These observations vary between searches and are reported separately from measured website visits.

FAQ

AI SEO services in Patna — FAQs.

How much do AI SEO services in Patna cost?

We quote after reviewing your site, locations and priority services. A single-location audit has a different scope from rewriting a catalogue or correcting several branch listings. Your proposal specifies the page count, fees and reporting frequency.

Can you work with our existing website developer?

Yes. We can deliver URL-specific briefs, suggested copy and acceptance checks for your developer, then review the published changes.

Do I need a separate page for every Patna neighbourhood?

Create one when you can provide distinct information, such as a staffed branch, a delivery zone or different appointment hours. A single coverage section often answers the question for businesses serving several areas from one address.

Can you guarantee mentions in ChatGPT or Google AI results?

No agency controls those selections. Our work focuses on accessible pages, accurate business information and answers backed by evidence. Your report identifies exactly what we changed and what we observed.

What should I send for the initial review?

Send your website URL, business listing, main service area and one example of a customer enquiry you want more often. Please remove personal customer details before sharing examples.
