FAQ

On page off page seo Patna — FAQs.

What is the difference between on-page and off-page SEO?

On-page SEO is everything you optimise on your own website — content, keywords, titles, structure and internal links. Off-page SEO is activity elsewhere that builds authority, such as backlinks, citations, reviews and brand mentions.

Which is more important?

Both. On-page SEO makes you relevant; off-page SEO makes you trusted. In competitive Patna categories you need both to reach page one.

Do you use paid or spammy backlinks?

No. We build links through genuine content, local partnerships, PR and directories, which protects your site from Google penalties.
