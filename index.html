FAQ

Questions, answered.

What digital marketing services does Zyrostyle offer?

Zyrostyle offers SEO, content strategy, content creation, social media marketing and paid advertising. We also help businesses structure website content for search engines and answer-based discovery.

How much does digital marketing cost in Patna?

Pricing depends on the services, deliverables, campaign scope and advertising budget. Request a proposal that separates agency fees, content production and advertising spend so you can compare costs clearly.

Should my business start with SEO or Google Ads?

SEO builds organic search visibility over time. Google Ads can reach people searching for your services once campaigns are approved and running, but requires an advertising budget. The right starting point depends on demand, your website’s readiness, budget and how quickly you need enquiries.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is an ongoing process, and results vary with competition, website condition and the work required. Early improvements may appear before meaningful lead growth. A proposal should explain priorities, milestones and reporting without guaranteeing rankings or a fixed results date.

Do you work with businesses outside Patna?

Zyrostyle serves businesses in Patna and across Bihar, with remote collaboration available for clients elsewhere in India. Discuss your location, audience and service requirements during the initial consultation.

Do you create content in Hindi and English?

Yes. Zyrostyle creates content in English, Hindi and Hinglish. The language and format should reflect your audience, brand voice and the channels where the content will appear.
